Inside The Madness

Set inside and beyond Arkham, the film follows Arthur as he wrestles with his public legend and private unraveling. Opposite him is Lee, played by Lady Gaga, whose fascination with Joker amplifies the mania. Their bond becomes a duet of obsession, identity, and destruction.
