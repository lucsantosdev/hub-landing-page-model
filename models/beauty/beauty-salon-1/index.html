About Our Labspa

Founded in 2010, Labspa was born from a passion for beauty, wellness, and self-care. Located in the heart of New York, our boutique spa has grown into a trusted haven for hundreds of loyal clients seeking a moment of calm in their busy lives. Our team of certified beauty professionals brings years of expertise across cosmetics, nail care, and hair styling — always using cruelty-free, high-quality products. At Labspa, we believe that looking good starts with feeling good, and every visit is designed to leave you glowing inside and out.
